Frequently asked questions

How big is the studio?

We are 25 people: designers, strategists and producers who work together on every project.

How long does a project take?

A brand identity usually takes 6 to 8 weeks and a website 8 to 12 weeks. We agree a timeline before we start.

How do we get started?

Send us a message with the contact form. We reply within one working day and arrange a short call.

Can you work with our existing brand?

Yes. We can extend an existing brand into new products, pages and print, or refresh it step by step.
